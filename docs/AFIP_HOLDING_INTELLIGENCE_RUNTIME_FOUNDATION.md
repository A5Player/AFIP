# AFIP Milestone W Pack W7 — Holding Intelligence Runtime Foundation

W7 adds advisory-only holding intelligence after W6.

It evaluates trend continuity, structure integrity, regime stability,
momentum, exit evidence, MFE, MAE, unrealized profit, protected profit,
holding duration, news risk, and spread abnormality.

Advisory results:

- WAIT_DATA
- HOLD
- PROTECT_PROFIT
- REDUCE_EXPOSURE
- EXIT_REVIEW

W7 never modifies, partially closes, or closes an order. Execution and MT5
authority remain outside this module.
