# AFIP Trading Plan Selection Foundation

Milestone W Pack W4 converts W3 strategy candidates into ranked plan candidates.

It checks plan compatibility, strategy score, evidence count, sample size, and evidence quality. It fails closed whenever an input or threshold is not valid.

The maximum result is `ELIGIBLE_FOR_OQS_REVIEW`. W4 has no final decision, execution, order-send, lot, or final SL/TP authority and cannot bypass any AFIP gate. Plan templates are central research/intelligence definitions and are not coupled to P1-P4.
